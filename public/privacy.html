Baleen

A small companion for the pages you read, with no server of her own. Here is how you use her, and what happens to your data.

01

How to use

  • She lives on the pages you browse. Nothing starts on its own — you ask, and she answers.
  • Highlight a section and open its chat to ask about that part of the page. Or use Summarize for the page’s key points, and Keywords for its research terms.
  • Drag her anywhere, resize her, change her outfit or theme from the popup, and press Alt+Shift+G (Option+Shift+G on a Mac) to hide or show her.

02

What happens to your data

  • Nothing is uploaded while you browse. Page text leaves your browser only when you send a question or pick Summarize or Keywords.
  • That text goes straight to DeepSeek at api.deepseek.com, with the model you chose, and DeepSeek handles it under its own privacy policy. It never passes through us, because there is no backend, no account, and no analytics.
  • Your API key is stored on your own device, is hidden once saved, and is used only to authorize those DeepSeek requests. Removing it in the popup stops all AI requests, and uninstalling the extension erases it.
  • Your chats stay in the tab they came from, so a reload restores them, and they disappear when you close the chat or the tab. Images are never sent — only captions and alt text, because DeepSeek is text-only.

Baleen is an independent project by Fungusan and is not an official DeepSeek plugin. This policy is also published at fungusan.com/baleen/privacy.
