Baleen privacy notice

A personal browser extension that shows a full-body companion on HTTP and HTTPS pages, answers questions about a section you highlight, and can summarize a page or list its keywords, using your own DeepSeek API key. Top-level PDFs open in a bundled reader with the same selection chat and Study actions.

What stays on your device

The extension does not read page text in the background and never watches what you type. It reads the page only when you act: the section you highlight when you open its chat, or the page's main readable text when you choose Keywords or Summarize. That text is not recorded, logged, or synced, and leaves the device only as the DeepSeek request you send. Her decorative animations respond only to what you do in her own chat blocks and Study panel, never to page content. Only the size, the companion's position, the chosen light/dark/system theme, the outfit, whether she is hidden, whether her animation plays, and the chosen model are saved in local extension storage. Choosing Copy in the Study panel writes that answer to your clipboard at your request; the extension does not store the draft or anything you copy. Section chats are kept in the tab's session storage, so a reload restores them; closing a block or the tab discards them, and Export chats is a download you trigger. PDFs opened in the bundled reader are fetched directly from the site, rendered inside the tab, and never uploaded; the extracted document text stays in memory only for the Study actions and the selection chat you trigger.

AI requests and your API key

Nothing is sent to DeepSeek automatically. When you highlight a section and send a prompt, the extension sends your question, the selected text, the page title, any image captions or alt text found in that section, and the earlier questions and answers in the same section block. When you choose Keywords or Summarize in the Study panel, it sends the page's main readable text, the page title, and built-in instructions; in the PDF reader that text comes from the document itself. Requests go to https://api.deepseek.com using the API key you saved and the model you chose (DeepSeek Flash or DeepSeek V4 Pro); DeepSeek processes them under its own terms and privacy policy. The DeepSeek API is text-only, so image pixels are never sent: only captions and alt text are included, and the chat block says so when a selection contains images. Replies are shown in the block or panel and kept in the tab's session storage so a reload can restore them; they leave the device only as history on your next question, and they disappear when the block or tab closes. Your key is kept in local extension storage, is never displayed after saving, and is only sent to DeepSeek as an authorization header. The extension has no backend, account, or proxy of its own. Removing the key in the popup stops all AI requests.

Permissions and control

Storage saves preferences and your DeepSeek key. Her content script matches HTTP and HTTPS pages so she can appear without any domain to assign; this is what produces the browser's site-access notice. The same HTTP and HTTPS site access, plus the scripting permission, lets the extension re-inject that same companion script into already-open tabs after the extension is reloaded or updated, so she does not disappear until each page is refreshed. No page content is read through that mechanism. The webRequest permission only observes top-level HTTP(S) responses to detect a PDF content type and switch that tab to the bundled reader; it does not read page content, block requests, or watch other tabs' traffic. The content security policy allows network requests to api.deepseek.com, to the site whose PDF you opened, and to the extension's own packaged files; there is no active-tab lookup or browsing-history permission. Dragging her stores one shared position for every page. The popup's Show on pages switch or Alt+Shift+G (Option+Shift+G on a Mac) hides or restores her everywhere. Revoking site access hides her on that page. Uninstalling removes extension-local settings and the saved key.

External services

No analytics, telemetry, backend, remote font, remote artwork, or remote audio. The only network requests are the DeepSeek call you trigger with your own key and, when you open a PDF in the reader, fetching that PDF from the site you navigated to. Bundled images are exposed to web pages solely so the companion can render; this is not page-reading permission. The underlying chat website continues under its own privacy policy.
